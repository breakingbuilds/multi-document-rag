About Us

Aurora Dynamics (Pvt.) Ltd. was founded in 2015 in Lahore, Pakistan, by Ayesha Rahman and Bilal Khan. What started as a five-person data science studio is now a team of 180 engineers, consultants and designers serving clients in South Asia, the Middle East and Europe.

Our mission is to make advanced AI practical, reliable and affordable for mid-sized businesses. We are ISO 27001 certified and a certified partner of the three major cloud providers.

Services

AI Consulting

Strategy, proof-of-concepts and production machine learning systems, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistants, forecasting models and computer vision.

Cloud Engineering

Cloud migration, Kubernetes platforms, infrastructure-as-code and 24/7 managed operations.

Data Engineering

Modern data platforms, streaming pipelines, data warehouses and governance.

Custom Software Development

Web and mobile product development with dedicated, cross-functional teams.
