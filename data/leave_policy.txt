AURORA DYNAMICS - LEAVE POLICY
Version 3.1 - Effective 1 January 2026
Owner: People Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains the types of leave available to employees of Aurora Dynamics, how much leave
each employee is entitled to, and how leave is requested and approved. It applies to all permanent
employees in Lahore, Karachi and Dubai.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every permanent employee is entitled to 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues
monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month of service and is credited to the employee's PeopleHub
balance at the start of each month.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Annual leave cannot be
encashed except at the time of final settlement.

Annual leave requests must be submitted in PeopleHub at least 5 working days in advance. Requests
for more than 5 consecutive days require 2 weeks' notice. Line managers approve or decline requests
within 2 working days.

3. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick leave days per year. Sick leave must be reported to the line
manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any
absence of more than 2 consecutive working days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

4. CASUAL LEAVE
Employees receive 6 casual leave days per year for urgent personal matters. Casual leave may be
taken for a maximum of 2 consecutive days and should be requested at least one day in advance where
possible.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
Maternity leave: 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Employees should inform People Operations at least 8
weeks before the expected due date.
Paternity leave: 2 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.
Adoption leave: 4 weeks of fully paid leave for the primary carer.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The Company observes the public holidays announced in the annual government gazette for each office
location (typically 12 to 14 days). The holiday calendar is published on PeopleHub every January.

7. UNPAID LEAVE
Employees who have exhausted their paid leave may apply for unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year.
Unpaid leave requires approval from the Head of People Operations.

8. LEAVE DURING PROBATION
Annual leave accrues during probation but may only be taken with the line manager's approval and
normally not before the completion of the first 2 months of service. Sick leave is available from
day one.

9. HOW TO REQUEST LEAVE
All leave is requested through the PeopleHub portal: My Profile > Leave > New Request. The request
goes to the line manager for approval and the employee is notified by email. Leave balances are
visible on the PeopleHub dashboard at any time.
